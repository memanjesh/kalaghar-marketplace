KalaGhar Export Hub

निर्यात केन्द्र · For artisans and cooperatives selling abroad

Selling to customers in the USA, Europe or Australia shouldn't mean learning customs forms. Bring your international orders to our hub in Kathmandu and we take care of the rest.

How it works

  1. You get an order from abroad in your seller dashboard. It's marked "International — drop at Export Hub".
  2. Drop it off at the hub, or book a pickup inside Kathmandu Valley. From outside the valley, send it to us by Nepal Can Move or Pathao — the cost is covered by the buyer's shipping charge.
  3. We check quality against your listing photos, so buyers get what they expect and you avoid returns.
  4. We pack for travel with protective packaging for statues, singing bowls and thangkas.
  5. We do the paperwork: customs declaration, HS codes, invoice, and a Certificate of Origin where it helps the buyer save on import duty.
  6. We ship and track by EMS or express courier, combining many artisans' parcels to get better rates. Tracking goes to the buyer automatically.
  7. You get paid when the buyer receives the order, to your eSewa, Khalti or bank account.

What you pay

Nothing extra. Hub handling is included in KalaGhar's commission, and the buyer pays international shipping at checkout.

Good to know

  • Antiques, items over 100 years old, and religious objects of heritage value need Department of Archaeology clearance and can't be exported through the hub.
  • Wildlife products (ivory, shahtoosh, certain furs and bones) are prohibited.
  • Food items like tea and spices must be sealed and labelled with ingredients and best-before date.
