Privacy Policy

Effective ·

We respect your privacy and follow Nepal's Individual Privacy Act, 2075 and the Electronic Commerce Act, 2081. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

InformationWhy we need it
Name, email, phone, delivery addressTo process and deliver orders, send invoices and order updates
Gift recipient's name, phone and addressTo deliver gifts you send; used only for that delivery
Order history, messages with sellers, reviewsTo provide your account, resolve problems and show verified reviews
Payment confirmation (reference number, status)To confirm payment. We never see or store your card number, PIN or wallet password — these go directly to Khalti, eSewa, Fonepay, ConnectIPS or our card processor.
Seller details: ID or cooperative registration, workshop photos, licences, payout walletTo verify artisans, show badges and pay sellers
Basic usage data: pages and products viewed, items favourited or added to bagTo show sellers anonymous shop statistics and improve the site

What we don't do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not publish your testimonials, photos or messages without your consent. Reviews show only your first name.
  • We do not send marketing emails unless you subscribe; every newsletter has an unsubscribe link.

Who we share it with

  • The seller of your order receives your name, delivery address and phone so they can ship it.
  • Couriers (Nepal Can Move, Pathao, postal and express partners) receive what they need to deliver.
  • Payment providers process payments under their own privacy policies.
  • Authorities, when Nepali law requires it.

How we protect it

The site uses HTTPS encryption. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes, never in plain text. Customer data is encrypted in our database, and only staff who need it can access it.

How long we keep it

Order and invoice records are kept for as long as tax law requires. Account data is kept while your account is open; you can ask us to delete it at any time, except records we must legally keep.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, download or delete your personal information, or withdraw consent for marketing. Email or use the complaint form (choose "Privacy / my data"). We respond within 15 days.

Cookies and local storage

We store small amounts of data in your browser to remember your language, currency, theme, bag and favourites. We do not use advertising trackers.

Children

KalaGhar is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their information.
