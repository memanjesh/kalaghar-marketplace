Return & Refund Policy

Effective

In short: if your item arrives faulty, damaged, wrong or not as described, tell us within days of delivery and you'll get an exchange or a full refund, including shipping. Refunds are processed within 3–5 business days after we confirm the return.

Items that can't be returned for change of mind

  • Personalised or made-to-order items
  • Tea, spices and other food
  • Earrings and items worn on the body, for hygiene
  • Items damaged after delivery through use

These can still be returned if they arrive faulty, damaged, wrong or not as described.

Natural variation in handmade goods

Hand-dyed, hand-woven and hand-cast pieces vary slightly from photos and from each other. Small differences are not a defect, but a clearly different item is.

How to request a return or refund

  1. Go to My Account → My orders and press Report a problem, or message the seller from the order. You can also use the complaint form.
  2. Tell us what happened and add a photo. Please keep the item and packaging.
  3. We reply within 1 business day with return instructions (a courier pickup inside Nepal, or a prepaid label from abroad where needed).
  4. Once the return is checked, we refund you within 3–5 business days to your original payment method. Cash-on-delivery orders are refunded to your eSewa, Khalti or bank account.

Cancellations

Orders can be cancelled for a full refund until the seller ships them. If a payment fails but money was deducted, it is refunded within 7 days.
