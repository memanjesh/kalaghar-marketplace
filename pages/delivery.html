Delivery times and charges

Shipping is calculated from the parcel's packed weight and shown in full at checkout before you pay. Free delivery inside Nepal on orders over .

Times are counted from dispatch. Artisans dispatch most items within 1–3 business days; made-to-order and personalised pieces may need 3–7 extra days, shown on the listing.

Within Nepal

  • Delivered by Nepal Can Move or Pathao, with tracking and SMS updates.
  • Cash on delivery is available within Nepal.
  • Gifts sent from abroad to Nepal can include a gift message and a packing slip without prices.

International

  • International parcels go through the KalaGhar Export Hub in Kathmandu, where they are checked, packed for travel and sent with tracking by postal EMS or express courier.
  • We prepare the customs declaration and, where useful, a Certificate of Origin for Nepali handicrafts.
  • Import duties and taxes in your country are set by your government and are not included in our price. The courier may collect them on delivery.
  • Optional shipping insurance ( of the item value) is available at checkout and recommended for statues, thangkas and other fragile or valuable items.

Delays

If your order will be late, we tell you before the estimated date. Festival periods (Dashain and Tihar) and weather on mountain roads can add a few days. If an order hasn't arrived 7 days after its estimated date, report it from your account and it is covered by Purchase Protection.

Tracking

When an artisan ships, the tracking number appears in My Account → My orders and in your email updates.

Wrong address or failed delivery

Please check your address and phone at checkout. If a parcel comes back because of a wrong address or because nobody could be reached, we contact you to arrange re-delivery; extra shipping may apply.
