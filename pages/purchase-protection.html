KalaGhar Purchase Protection

For buyers and sellers · Effective

Your money is safe until your order arrives. When you pay, the money goes to KalaGhar, not straight to the seller. We release it to the artisan only after your order is delivered as described.

How it works

  1. You pay with Khalti, eSewa, Fonepay, ConnectIPS or an international card. KalaGhar holds the payment.
  2. The artisan ships and adds a tracking number.
  3. You receive it and press "I received it" in My Account, or the order completes automatically days after delivery.
  4. The artisan is paid their share of the sale.

What's covered

  • The order never arrived (7 or more days after the estimated delivery date)
  • It arrived damaged
  • It's significantly not as described, or the wrong item was sent

Coverage is up to per order, including shipping. For more valuable items, choose shipping insurance at checkout.

How to open a case

In My Account → My orders, press Report a problem within days of the estimated delivery date. Add a photo if you can. While we review, the seller's payout is frozen. Most cases are resolved within 5 business days with a refund, replacement or return.

For sellers

You're protected too. If you shipped on time with valid tracking, the item matches your listing, and you replied to the buyer within 48 hours, we review the evidence fairly before any refund. Cases are decided by a person on our team, never automatically.

Not covered

  • Change of mind (see our Return & Refund Policy)
  • Small natural variations in handmade items
  • Import duties charged by your country
  • Payments made outside KalaGhar, for example directly to a seller's wallet
